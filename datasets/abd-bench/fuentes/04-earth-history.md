## D01 — Vela incident, 22 Sep 1979 (South Atlantic flash)

- Observers:
  - O1: Vela Hotel satellite OPS 6911 bhangmeters (orbital optical). y1 = double-flash light curve, 2–3 kt signature at ~47°S 40°E.
  - O2: Ruina panel / White House science advisers (lab-calibration frame). y2 = anomalous light-signature deviation → "probably not nuclear".
  - O3: NRL hydroacoustic + AFTAC analysts (ocean-acoustic frame). y3 = signals "unique to nuclear shots in maritime environment" near Prince Edward/Marion Islands.
- Conflict: O1 says nuclear-like flash; O2 says instrument/zoo event (micrometeoroid); O3 + CIA panel says underwater nuclear-consistent signals.
- H per-observer: H1 = atmospheric nuclear test (Israel/South Africa joint). H2 = spurious zoo signal / sensor aging. H3 = shallow-water nuclear shot.
- Wrong inductive: "no radionuclide debris ⇒ no test" (absence of fallout treated as disproof; fallout collection was sparse/downwind-missing).
- Ground truth: consensus since 2016 declassifications (Smith files, CIA panel) = low-yield atmospheric nuclear test, likely Israeli with South African assistance. Residual: no official claimant; some data still classified.

## D02 — Loss of USS Scorpion, May 1968 (acoustic search)

- Observers:
  - O1: SOSUS Atlantic arrays (narrowband tracking frame). y1 = nothing found (below threshold for implosion transient).
  - O2: Columbia Univ. Canary-Islands hydrophone station, Gordon Hamilton (broadband missile-impact frame). y2 = breakup-noise sequence taped.
  - O3: AFTAC "O" stations (broadband point-source frame). y3 = 15 transients over ~3 min, first explosive event 1842 22 May + bulkhead implosions.
- Conflict: Navy's own SOSUSgrams showed nothing → "no acoustic fix possible"; two peripheral broadband stations heard the whole death sequence.
- H per-observer: H1 = lost without trace / Soviet action elsewhere (dead-reckoning search box). H2/H3 = onboard explosion → 180° turn → descent past crush depth → implosions; wreck lies EAST of datum (Craven Bayesian offset).
- Wrong inductive: "SOSUS hears everything in the Atlantic ⇒ silence means no event in coverage" (frame mismatch: narrowband tracker blind to broadband transient).
- Ground truth: wreck found 28 Oct 1968 by USNS Mizar at ~11,000 ft, ~400 mi SW of Azores, where Craven's acoustic triangulation + Bayesian search put it. Cause of initial explosion still debated (torpedo malfunction leading theory).

## D03 — Tunguska, 30 Jun 1908 (eyewitnesses vs barometers)

- Observers:
  - O1: Evenki + Vanavara witnesses (Semenov at 65 km: sky split, heat, thrown meters). y1 = local fire-pillar, sound moving east→north.
  - O2: Irkutsk/Tashkent/Tbilisi/Jena seismographs. y2 = small-earthquake trace, no crater epicenter.
  - O3: Whipple/Kew microbarographs (Shaw-Dines, SE England). y3 = anomalous wave train, air-wave speed ~320 m/s, circled globe twice; Whipple 1930 two-component structure.
- Conflict: witnesses describe a falling burning body → expect meteorite crater; instruments show sky-coupled pressure/seismic energy with NO impact crater (Kulik expeditions found flattened 2,150 km² forest, no crater).
- H per-observer: H1 = fallen "Great Siberian Meteorite" / divine fire. H2 = tectonic quake. H3 = distant giant explosion of unknown source.
- Wrong inductive: "big explosion ⇒ crater ⇒ meteorite on the ground" (impact-frame projection; energy was deposited 5–10 km up).
- Ground truth: ~50–60 m stony asteroid airburst, 3–50 Mt (modern ~10–15 Mt), burst altitude; barograph energy matches Bikini-scale (~15 Mt) at equal range. Comet vs asteroid composition still open.
- URLs:

## D04 — 1906 San Francisco quake → elastic rebound

- Observers:
  - O1: Field geologists (Lawson commission): 470 km surface rupture, fences/roads offset meters. y1 = fault slipped during the quake.
  - O2: Bay-area triangulation resurveys (USCGS 1850s–1906, Gold-Rush navigation baselines). y2 = far-field shear accumulated for decades BEFORE the quake, released coseismically.
  - O3: Intensity mappers: MMI VII–IX correlated with sediment/fill, not distance to rupture. y3 = shaking damage map ≠ slip map.
- Conflict: common sense (O1) says quake = sudden underground explosion causing slip; geodesy (O2) shows slow pre-strain mirrored by coseismic release — the "cause" preceded the event by decades.
- H per-observer: H1 = local rupture as cause. H2 (Reid 1910) = elastic rebound: centuries of far-field loading, seconds of release. H3 = site-condition amplifier.
- Wrong inductive: "damage intensity ∝ proximity to cause" (ignores path/site projection; strongest shaking on bay fill far from hard-rock rupture).
- Ground truth: Reid's elastic-rebound theory (1910), from 1906 displacements; principal earthquake-cycle model to this day. Death toll revised 700 → 3,000+.

## D05 — Magnetic stripes → seafloor spreading (Vine–Matthews–Morley 1963)

- Observers:
  - O1: Ship-towed magnetometers (Mason, Raff, Vine): symmetric lineated anomalies flanking mid-ocean ridges. y1 = zebra stripes, ~20 km wide.
  - O2: Fixist field geologists: continents fixed, anomalies = local ore bodies / intrusions. y2 = stripes as static crustal heterogeneity.
  - O3: Paleomagnetists (Cox et al. 1964 land lavas): geomagnetic field reverses quasi-periodically (~0.5–1 Myr). y3 = reversal timescale.
- Conflict: O2 reads stripes as permanent local geology; O1+O3 read them as a tape recording of time — symmetric about the ridge, impossible under static crust.
- H per-observer: H1 (Vine–Matthews) = ridge "twin-headed tape recorder": magma cools through Curie point, locks in contemporary polarity, spreads at ~cm/yr. H2 = ad-hoc local sources. H3 initially unconnected timescale.
- Wrong inductive: "symmetric pattern ⇒ symmetric static source bodies" (projects a time process onto a space-only frame).
- Ground truth: VMM hypothesis (Nature 199, 947–949, 1963) became the first decisive test of Hess/Dietz seafloor spreading and plate tectonics; Pitman–Heirtzler Pacific-Antarctic profile confirmed symmetry. Spreading rates (~4 cm/yr) computed from stripe width × reversal period.

## D06 — Tide gauges vs GPS/VLBI: sea level or sinking land?

- Observers:
  - O1: Coastal tide gauges (century records). y1 = relative sea level rising ~1.8 mm/yr globally (20th c.).
  - O2: Co-located continuous GPS / VLBI vertical velocities. y2 = land itself moving ±mm/yr (GIA rebound in ex-glaciated zones, subsidence from sediment compaction, groundwater withdrawal, tectonics).
  - O3: Satellite altimetry (absolute sea level, ~1993+). y3 = geocentric rise, too short for centennial trends, confirms gauge+GPS decomposition.
- Conflict: gauges in Scandinavia show sea level FALLING; gauges in subsiding deltas show catastrophic rise — same ocean, opposite signs. Without land-motion correction, "global rise" is frame-contaminated.
- H per-observer: H1 = ocean volume change only. H2 = vertical land motion (GIA + local subsidence) as hidden additive. H3 = absolute reference.
- Wrong inductive: "tide gauge measures the sea" (it measures sea-minus-land; outside formerly glaciated zones GIA correction alone correlates poorly with GPS — Houston & Dean 2012, 147 sites).
- Ground truth: decomposition RSL = ASL − VLM; GPS-corrected gauges recover ~1.8 ± 0.5 mm/yr 20th-c. rise (Bouin & Wöppelmann 2010); GPS vertical accurate to ~1 mm/yr. Local VLM (not GIA models) is the required correction.

## D07 — GW150914: coincident chirp vs environmental glitch (14 Sep 2015)

- Observers:
  - O1: LIGO Hanford (H1) strain channel. y1 = 0.2-s sweep 35→250 Hz, peak strain 1e-21, matched-filter SNR ~20.
  - O2: LIGO Livingston (L1) strain channel, 3000 km away. y2 = same chirp morphology, SNR ~13, arriving 6.9 ms earlier (light-travel-consistent delay).
  - O3: Environmental veto array (seismometers, magnetometers, lightning network) + data-quality flags. y3 = no coincident disturbance; χ² ≈ 1 (template matches data, not a glitch).
- Conflict: each single-detector trigger could be an instrumental glitch (LIGO glitches are common); neither alone claims detection. Coincidence within 10 ms + identical chirp + clean vetoes converts two weak projections into one 5.1σ event (false-alarm < 1/203,000 yr).
- H per-observer: H1/H2 alone = local noise transient. H* = binary black-hole merger (36+29 → 62 M⊙, 3 M⊙ radiated, 410 Mpc) sweeping both sites at light speed.
- Wrong inductive: "loud in one detector ⇒ astrophysical" (single-detector loudness is glitch-dominated; significance comes only from inter-site coherence + veto silence).
- Ground truth: first direct GW detection + first binary-black-hole merger observed; PRL 116, 061102 (11 Feb 2016). Blind hardware injections existed as a class but GW150914 was not one (injection system confirmed clean).

- Observers:
  - O1: U-2 pilot Heyser + NPIC photo-interpreters (Lundahl/Brugioni): 928 frames, ~3-ft resolution. y1 = six missile trailers, erector/launchers, tents, graded pads at San Cristóbal — SS-4 (R-12) MRBM signatures.
  - O2: Soviet diplomats + ground-level visitors in Cuba. y2 = "defensive aid only, no offensive weapons" (Dobrynin assurances; Gromyko to Kennedy 18 Oct).
  - O3: Follow-on low-level RF-101/Vought sorties + second U-2 passes. y3 = Sagua la Grande MRBM + Guanajay/Remedios IRBM sites — pattern replicates.
- Conflict: authoritative human assurances (O2) vs overhead technical evidence (O1); ExComm debate 16 Oct turned on which observer frame to trust.
- H per-observer: H1 = covert offensive MRBM/IRBM deployment (Operation Anadyr). H2 = defensive SAM/technician presence only. H3 confirms H1 pattern at multiple sites.
- Wrong inductive: "no ground-observable mobilization ⇒ no deployment" (Soviet maskirovka: missiles moved at night, site grading mimicked agricultural work; ground frame was denial-filtered).
- Ground truth: R-12 (SS-4, ~2,000 km range) sites under construction at San Cristóbal since mid-Oct; Kennedy briefed 16 Oct (Bundy held 15 Oct evening report overnight); 13-day crisis; Anderson's U-2 downed 27 Oct over same sites.
